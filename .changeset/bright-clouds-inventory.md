Add the standalone CloudInventory plugin with package-owned SDKs for AWS, Azure,
GCP, DigitalOcean, Oracle, Vultr, Linode, Hetzner, Alibaba Cloud, Cloudflare, and
MongoDB Atlas. Include snapshots, version history, diffs, scheduled discovery,
Terraform export, public driver loaders, and clean-package isolation checks.
